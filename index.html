Privacy Policy – World Cup 2026

Effective Date: June 6, 2026  |  Last Updated: June 6, 2026

World Cup 2026 ("we", "our", or "the app") is an unofficial companion app for following the 2026 football tournament — fixtures, group standings, the knockout bracket, team squads, top scorers, venues, and a personal match-prediction game. This policy explains what information the app and its third‑party services process, how it is used, and your choices. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with FIFA or any official body.

1. Summary at a Glance

  • No account, login, name, email, or precise location is collected by us.
  • Your saved predictions and settings stay on your device.
  • The app shows ads via Google AdMob (may use your Advertising ID).
  • It uses Firebase Analytics (usage), Crashlytics (crash reports), and Cloud Messaging (match notifications).
  • It fetches tournament data (scores, fixtures, squads) from our backend over the internet.
  • EEA/UK users are shown a consent choice before personalized ads.

2. Information Stored On Your Device

The following is stored locally and is not transmitted to us:

  • Saved predictions — your bracket picks and chosen champion (local database).
  • Preferences — theme, favourite team, match‑time display, notification toggle, and the backend server address.
  • Cached tournament data — a temporary network cache so the app works offline and loads faster.
  • Ad frequency counters (to limit how often full‑screen ads appear).

This data is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

3. Information Processed by Third‑Party Services

The app relies on the services below. Each may collect limited technical/usage data as described in their own privacy policies.

3.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

The app displays banner, native, and interstitial ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). AdMob may collect your Advertising ID (resettable), IP address, device information, coarse (inferred) location, and ad interaction/performance data for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

See the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings, or at adssettings.google.com.

3.2 Firebase Analytics (Usage)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate (e.g. screens viewed, features used such as viewing a team or saving a prediction). This typically includes app instance identifiers, device/OS information, and approximate region. It is used to improve the app and is not used to identify you personally.

3.3 Firebase Crashlytics (Stability)

If the app crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects a crash report containing device state, OS version, and a stack trace so we can diagnose and fix the issue. It does not collect the personal data listed in section 5.

3.4 Firebase Cloud Messaging (Notifications)

To deliver match notifications (kick‑offs, goals, full‑time results, daily fixtures), the app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging. Google assigns a messaging token to the app installation, and the app subscribes to a broadcast "matches" topic. We send the same notification to all subscribers — we do not build a per‑user profile from this. You can turn notifications off in the app's Settings or in Android system settings.

4. Network & Our Backend

The app downloads read‑only tournament content (teams, fixtures, live scores, standings, squads, head‑to‑head records, top scorers, venues) from our backend server. As with any internet request, our server may receive standard request metadata such as your IP address and device/network information. The app does not send your name, account, predictions, or other personal content to our backend.

5. What We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email, phone number, address, or account credentials.
  • No precise GPS location, contacts, calendar, photos, media, files, or messages.
  • No biometric, health, financial, or other sensitive personal data.

6. Advertising Consent (EEA / UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app presents a consent message (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before serving personalized ads, in line with GDPR and applicable rules. Where consent is required you can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → "Manage ad consent" inside the app. Depending on your choice, ads may be personalized or non‑personalized.

7. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: load tournament data and ads, and detect connectivity.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: show optional match notifications (Android 13+ runtime permission).
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: access the Advertising ID for AdMob (added automatically by the ads SDK).

8. Data Retention

  • On‑device data (predictions, preferences, cache) is kept until you clear storage or uninstall.
  • Analytics, crash, advertising, and messaging data are retained by Google per their respective policies.

9. Your Rights & Choices

  • Ads: reset/limit your Advertising ID in Android Settings; change ad consent via Settings → "Manage ad consent" (where applicable).
  • Notifications: disable in the app's Settings or in Android system settings.
  • Local data: delete saved predictions in the app, or clear the app's storage / uninstall to remove all local data.
  • EEA/UK (GDPR): you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, and objection regarding data processed by Google's services — exercise these through Google's tools and policies.
  • US state privacy (e.g. CCPA): we do not "sell" personal information. Advertising data sharing can be limited via the controls above.

10. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can address it.

11. Security

  • On‑device data remains within Android's standard app sandbox.
  • Network requests to our backend and to Google services use HTTPS.
  • We do not operate any custom remote store of your personal data.

12. Third Parties

  • Google AdMob — ad delivery and measurement.
  • Google Firebase — Analytics, Crashlytics, and Cloud Messaging.
  • See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google processes the above.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect feature or legal changes. The "Last Updated" date will change on revision; material changes will be highlighted where practical.
